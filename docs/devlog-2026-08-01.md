# Development log — 2026-08-01

## PingoWolf Alpha 1 release qualification

1. The Author selected `pingowolf` as the durable combined-product branch in
   the `bgates747/agon-vdp` fork. The independent
   `experiment/hecker-rasterizer` branch remains Pingo development and is not
   being published as the combined product.
2. The release identity is **PingoWolf 0.1.0 Alpha 1**, with tag
   `pingowolf-v0.1.0-alpha.1`. The base remains Agon Platform VDP 2.16.0
   Bistromathics, while Pingo and Wolf3DOrig print their independent
   `0.1.0 Alpha 1` subsystem identities on separate lines.
3. The physical-hardware release fixture was installed at `/PingoWolf` on the
   Agon SD card. Earth Party Flat ran first; the accepted audio-enabled
   Wolf3DOrig application ran second. The Author reported that the combined
   sequence worked beautifully. This accepts rendering, sequential subsystem
   use, Wolf gameplay/audio, and return-to-MOS behavior for the packaged
   fixture identities.
4. The release firmware is the same ordinary image accepted on hardware:
   1,118,096 bytes, SHA-256
   `b6c529de07f46a6d9d75da0c87908b75dd81b3cbdee4f4aaa4a774522c2ed2ce`.
5. Release preparation reran and passed the 106-path source-integration
   preflight; the ordinary native Pingo, Wolf, and cross-subsystem suites; the
   diagnostic suite; ASan/UBSan bridge and cross-subsystem tests; explicit
   clean builds of all three embedded environments; and the fail-closed
   headless combined emulator sequence. The clean diagnostic and unlit images
   also reproduced their qualified hashes exactly.
6. The release packages the immutable Wolf application accepted in the
   combined emulator and on hardware, SHA-256
   `2d845e2882ae22012f0c64c7705d47b7413dc6b6301bc07bcdfdfadbe635f1c7`,
   rather than silently substituting a later mutable Wolf build.
7. Deterministic hardware packaging is owned by
   `agon-dev-env/integrations/wolf_pingo_vdp`. It produces separate firmware
   and application ZIPs plus an all-in-one hardware ZIP, records source and
   artifact provenance, verifies qualified input hashes, extracts and
   byte-verifies its own output, and supplies an example autoexec without ever
   replacing a user's `autoexec.txt`.
8. No emulator binary is published in Alpha 1. The available executable was
   built on Pop!_OS and is not being presented as a portable Linux release.
9. Repeated physical heap/PSRAM exhaustion and recovery measurement remains
   unperformed. This is retained as an explicit alpha limitation and future
   hardening gate; it is not misrepresented as completed by the successful
   functional hardware sequence.
